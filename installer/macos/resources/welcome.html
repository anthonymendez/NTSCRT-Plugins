NTSCRT plug-in

This installer downloads the latest release of the NTSCRT plug-ins from github.com/anthonymendez/NTSCRT-Plugins and installs them for the apps it finds:

  • DaVinci Resolve 21.1.2+: OpenFX plug-in in /Library/OFX/Plugins
  • Final Cut Pro 12.4+: NTSCRT.app in /Applications (opened once to register the effect), only when the release includes it. The Final Cut plug-in isn't released yet, so for now this installs the Resolve plug-in.

It needs an internet connection. Quit DaVinci Resolve and Final Cut Pro first, or restart them afterwards.
